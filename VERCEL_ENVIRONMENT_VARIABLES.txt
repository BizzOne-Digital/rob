AFTER DEPLOYMENT - SQUARE WEBHOOK SETUP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to: https://developer.squareup.com/apps
2. Select your application
3. Go to "Webhooks" section
4. Click "Add webhook endpoint"
5. Enter: https://yourdomain.com/api/webhooks/square
6. Select these events:
   - payment.created
   - payment.updated
   - order.created
   - order.updated
7. Copy the "Signature Key" 
8. Add it to Vercel: SQUARE_WEBHOOK_SIGNATURE_KEY=<signature-key>
